**What it means:** combos are priced 2-10¢ above the product of the legs, so the market already accounts for most of the correlation. Our paper run (30 of 33 won, +$98) assumed the product price, so **it overstated the edge**. Version 1, which only bought at product + 1¢, never got a fill.

**Combi V2 (live, small).** Same signal: all four coins the same way, every leg ≥85¢, 6 to 3 minutes before close. But it now compares the **real quote plus fee** against how often combos in that price band actually won in our 45-day history (2,090 signals), and only buys if the quote is below that. With these six quotes, it would have bought one (87.9¢ against a historical 90.5%). So any edge left is small, a few cents, and depends on the history being well calibrated, which is exactly what failed us before.
