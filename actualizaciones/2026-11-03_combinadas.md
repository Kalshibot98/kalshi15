**Update: a new idea we're testing, combos (parlays) on the 15-min crypto markets**

Your comments pushed us toward the real issue: our losses come in clusters, because the coins move together. So we flipped it around and asked whether that correlation can be the edge instead of the risk.

Kalshi lets you buy a combo of BTC + ETH + SOL + XRP for the same 15-minute window. In our data, the 15-min markets of different coins finish the same way 64% of the time (BTC and ETH 83%). If a combo is priced near the product of its legs, as if they were independent, a combo of four correlated favorites is underpriced.

**The rule:** between 6 and 3 minutes before close, if all four coins point the same way and every leg's favored side costs ≥85¢, buy the combo: $20, $30 or $50 depending on how strong the legs are. One per window.

**So far (simulation, not real money):**
- Backtest, 45 days: $500 → ~$5,180, 89% of combos won, worst drawdown 12%.
- Live paper run, first 17 hours: 27 combos, 24 won, **+$65 on $500 (+13%)**.

**The big caveat:** the simulation assumes the combo costs the product of the legs. In real Kalshi combos we've seen prices around 2.2× the product in general, and close to the product only when the legs are already ≥70¢ near the end, from a small sample. If the real price is closer to 2.2×, the edge is gone. So the next step is to check actual combo quotes at the moment we'd buy, and the window-clustering lesson from Rios applies here too: 17 hours is far from enough.
