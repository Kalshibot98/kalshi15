**Update 8: V5 gave back its gains overnight, so V6 starts betting *against* the combo in the middle price band**

First the bad news, as promised. V5 (late buys + price filter) ran overnight: **80 combos, 47 won, −$7.75**. The V5 balance went from about $31.60 back down to about $22. The small positive gap we saw at n=25 did not hold.

**What we noticed.** Combos whose YES cost between 40¢ and 65¢ kept being overpriced, both in our real fills and in our quote logger (quotes only, no buys). If YES is overpriced there, the other side, the combo's NO, should be underpriced. Kalshi's request for quote lets us buy that NO: a market maker who wants the YES sells us the NO.

**Combi V6, live since today with $1 per trade:**

| Combo YES price | What V6 does | When |
|---|---|---|
| under 40¢ | buys the YES (cheap) | only in the last 4 minutes |
| 40¢ to 65¢ | buys the combo's **NO** | from 14 minutes before the close |
| 65¢ and up | nothing | — |

- The NO is bought early, because our quote logger showed it doing best around 10 minutes out (about +$9 to +$11 over 50 simulated cases, quotes only, so take it with care).
- It skips the NO if YES + NO add up to more than $1.04, so we don't pay a big spread to the market maker.
- Still up to 4 combos per window, coins under 60% left out.
