**Update 6: V5 after 42 real fills, and why the next version buys later**

Combi V5 (mixed directions, drops or flips the weak coin, $1 per combo) has now run overnight with real money. Full ledger so far:

- **42 combos bought, 28 won, total −$4.77** on about $42 staked.

That alone isn't great. But when we split the fills by *when* the bot bought, the picture is very clear. V5 looks twice per 15-minute window: once with ~9 minutes left, once with ~6 minutes left.

| When it bought | Fills | Won | P&L |
|---|---|---|---|
| ~9 min before close | 17 | 8 (47%) | **−$5.51** |
| ~6 min before close | 25 | 20 (80%) | **+$0.74** |

Almost all the losses come from the early buys. With 9 minutes left there's still too much time for one coin to turn around, and the market maker's premium doesn't cover it. Three minutes later the leaders are much more settled.

**Next version:** same V5 logic, but it only buys in the later look, closer to the close. We'll keep the $1 size and post the results, good or bad.

Honest caveats: 42 fills is a small sample, the late buys are also pricier on average (so they win more but pay less per win), and +$0.74 on 25 fills is close to break-even, not a proven edge. What it does tell us is where *not* to buy.
