**Update 10: cutting back where we lose, keeping room where we think we win**

The idea behind this round of changes is simple: shrink the buys in the spots where we've been losing the most, and keep the ones where we think the edge is.

**Since Update 9 (this morning):** the cheap YES went 0 for 8 (−$8.00), the NO 1 for 3 (+$0.36). V7 as a whole now stands at:

| Side | Fills | Won | P&L |
|---|---|---|---|
| NO (YES at 70–90¢) | 49 | 15 | **+$11.89** |
| Cheap YES (under 40¢) | 31 | 7 | **−$10.98** |

The NO is carrying it; the cheap YES is eating almost all of it. So we changed three things:

**1. The cheap YES only on short combos.** Across all our real YES fills, combos of 2 coins (one up, one down) or 3 coins (two one way, one the other) made **+$8.30 on 26 fills**. Combos of 4 coins lost **−$15.60 on 48**, and in 32 of 54 YES losses only **one** coin failed. So now the YES is only bought as a 1-1 or 2-1 combo; if the base has 4 coins, the bot drops the weakest one.

**2. Skip the window after a clean sweep.** If the previous 15-minute window closed with all 4 coins going the same way, the bot buys nothing in the next one. After those windows our real buys (YES and NO) lost **−$23.90 on 70**.

**3. A new test: an early NO.** Right when a window opens (14.8 to 12 minutes before the close), the bot quotes the four "3 coins one way, 1 the other" combos and buys the NO of the one that pays best, only if it costs 95¢ or less. It wins small and often: so far 2 for 2, +$0.11. Honest warning: our quote logger says this NO wins about 96% of the time but still loses about 3.5¢ per $1, so it may turn out to be a slow loser. It's a separate test with its own ledger.

The NO filters from Update 9 stay: no NO from 6 am to noon ET, all coins the same way, NO at 15¢ or more.
