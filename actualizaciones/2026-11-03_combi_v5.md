**Update 5: Combi V5, mixed directions and dropping the weak coin**

V4 lasted about half an hour live: 2 fills at $1 (one won +$0.02, one lost −$1.00). Reviewing the losing combos gave us a better idea, so V5 replaced it.

**What we noticed.** In 3 of the 4 combos we lost on Nov 3, only **one** coin failed. The other three finished the way we bet. And those combos were the ones Kalshi quoted "cheap". Our guess: the cheap quote comes from that one coin, the one market makers see reversing. So instead of avoiding the whole combo, find the weak coin and work around it.

**How V5 works** (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP; two looks per 15-minute window, 9 to 6.5 and 6 to 3 minutes before close):
1. **Mixed directions.** Each coin goes the way its own order book points, up or down, so a combo can be "BTC up, ETH up, SOL down, XRP up". Coins sitting near 50/50 (favored side under 60¢) are left out.
2. **It asks for 9 real quotes at once:** the base combo, the four 3-coin combos that each drop one coin, and the four 4-coin combos that each flip one coin.
3. **Decision**, using the same premium measure as V4 (how much richer than the product of the legs, in odds):
   - If the base combo is quoted rich, it buys the base.
   - If not, the "weak coin" is the one whose removal leaves the richest 3-coin combo. It buys either that 3-coin combo or the 4-coin combo with the weak coin flipped, whichever is quoted richer, if it clears the bar.
   - Otherwise it skips.
4. **$1 per combo**, at most 40 a day. Every quote is logged, bought or not, so we can check later which choice was right.

**So far:** 1 fill, won (+$1.13 on a combo bought at 45¢). One trade proves nothing; we'll post the running results.
