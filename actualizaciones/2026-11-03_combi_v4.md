**Update 4: Combi V2 is off, Combi V4 is live with $1 bets to collect data**

Since Update 3 we went one step further and switched the live bot. Combi V2 is off: it lost $56.53 of our own money.

**What we noticed.** The combos that lost were the ones Kalshi sold cheap: the two quotes closest to the product of the legs (under 1¢ above it) both lost. The ones market makers charged clearly more for mostly won. Our reading: when someone is willing to sell a combo for less than the market usually charges, they probably see something we don't. When they charge a premium, they're protecting themselves against an outcome that is likely to happen.

**So V4 does the opposite of V2: it buys the "expensive" combos, at any probability.**
- Every 15-minute window it looks twice (9 to 6.5 and 6 to 3 minutes before close) at "all 4 up" and "all 4 down" for BTC, ETH, SOL and XRP. It no longer requires the legs to be favorites: any combo from 3% to 97% counts.
- It asks Kalshi for a real quote and buys only if the premium over the product of the legs is large, measured in odds so that low and high probabilities are treated the same: 10% quoted as 20%, or 90% quoted as 95%, both qualify.
- **$1 per combo**, at most 40 a day. The point is to buy information, not to make money yet.
- It logs every quote it gets, bought or not, so we can see which premium level actually wins.

**Honest numbers.** On the 28 real quotes we already had, V4's rule would have bought 23: 20 won, for roughly break-even (+$0.16 on $23). The new part is the low-probability combos (say 10% quoted at 20%), and there we have no data yet. That's what the $1 bets are for.
